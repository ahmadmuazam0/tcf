Our Mission

The Aim of The Core Four

Population is deficit in nutrients.
The reason is our food choices, eating behaviors, busy lifestyle.
Which ultimately compromise our health.
To manage this issue, there is need to develop energy as well as nutritious products, which should be organic yet easy to consume (ready to eat).
The aim of The Core Four is to develop such products which are healthy, tasteful, convenient, and nutritious.

Our Story

We're four university students who met through nutrition and food science courses. During our final year project, we noticed a gap between the calories people consume and the nutrients they actually need. Inspired to create something practical, we developed our first product—a functional bar formulated using USDA nutrition data to help close common nutrient gaps while keeping calories balanced.

Every ingredient is chosen based on scientific research, not trends. This bar is just the beginning. We're committed to studying other whole foods and developing more products that make it easier for people to meet their daily nutrition needs through real, data-driven solutions.
